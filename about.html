About Us

A community where every learner is seen, supported and challenged to reach further.

Empowering Young Minds from Day One

Taal High School was founded with one belief: great learning happens when students feel they belong. We combine strong academics, hands-on experiences and caring mentorship to prepare every student for college, work and life.

🎯

Our Mission

To provide quality basic education which will serve as their weapon and foundation for a life-long learnings and service for the common good.

🔭

Our Vision

Taal High School is an educational institution committed to discover full potential of every youth towards becoming responsible and productive members of society who are maka-Diyos, maka-Tao, maka-Kalikasan and maka-Bayan.

💡

Our Values

Excellence in the quality of education which will allow students access opportunies.
