About us

We are a school built around curious learners, committed teachers and families who take part in every step.

Our mission

To give every student a safe, caring place to learn, and the knowledge, skills and character to succeed in school, at work and in their community.

Our vision

A school where every learner grows into a confident, responsible and compassionate citizen who keeps learning and helps others do the same.

Excellence

We set high goals and support each student in reaching them.

Integrity

We are honest, fair and accountable in all we do.

Respect

We value every person and the community we share.
